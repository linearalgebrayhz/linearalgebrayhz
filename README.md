## Hi there 👋

My name is **Haozhe Yang**. I am a final-year undergraduate studying **Computer Science and Mathematics** at The University of Hong Kong.

My research interests lie in **multimodal generative modeling**, **spatial intelligence**, and **embodied AI**. I want to understand how generative models can build useful internal representations of dynamic physical worlds, rather than merely generate plausible observations of them.

In particular, I am interested in generative models that connect **language, vision, audio, motion, and physical dynamics**. I hope to explore how such models can move beyond producing perceptually plausible outputs toward understanding the structure, dynamics, and interactions of the world they model.

Several directions/topics I am interested in:

- Multimodal generative models for dynamic 3D/4D environments
- Spatial and physical intelligence
- Human motion and behavior modeling
- World models and embodied agents

Some questions I am currently interested in are:

- How can generative models learn representations of geometry, dynamics, and agent behavior that remain coherent over time?
- How can models integrate language, vision, speech, motion, and other modalities to understand and generate complex human behavior?
- How can generative world models move from predicting plausible observations toward supporting planning, interaction, and decision making in physical environments?
- What kinds of representations and training signals are needed for models to acquire grounded spatial and physical understanding rather than relying primarily on semantic or visual correlations?
